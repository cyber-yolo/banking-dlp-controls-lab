## Scope and limits

- Confirms that the outbound DLP pipeline detects IBANs typed in the message body.
- Confirms that the R2 lower bound (count 5) is inclusive.
- Not a TC scenario: no attachment and no SHA-256, so it does not validate
  attachment extraction (see TC-003b).
- Does not validate the block action: in TestWithoutNotifyUser mode, BlockAccess
  is not enforced.
